Meet the instructors

The following instructor profiles are fictional examples for this student website.

Maya Rivera

Maya leads gentle and restorative classes. Her teaching emphasizes clear explanations, optional props, and a comfortable pace for students beginning yoga.

Alex Morgan

Alex leads vinyasa classes and private sessions. Students are encouraged to ask questions and choose variations that suit their comfort and experience.

Accessibility and accommodations

Chair options, blocks, and straps can support different ways to participate. Students may rest or observe.

Please contact us before visiting to discuss entrance access, space requirements, or communication preferences. We will confirm arrangements before your session.

Studio policies

  • Arrive ten minutes early for your first class.
  • Respect personal space and silence your phone.
  • Hands-on assistance requires your consent.
  • Tell us as soon as possible if plans change.
  • Pause any movement that feels uncomfortable.
